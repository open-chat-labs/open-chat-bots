# Chess pieces

The pieces are JohnPablok's improved Cburnett chess set, from
[Chess Pieces and Board squares](https://opengameart.org/content/chess-pieces-and-board-squares)
on OpenGameArt, licensed under [CC-BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). They
are its "SVG No shadow" files, unchanged but for their names: a side (`w` or `b`) and the piece's
letter (`K`, `Q`, `R`, `B`, `N`, `P`).

Each is drawn to the scale of the set's 451-unit squares, so the pieces differ in size: a pawn's
smaller than a king. `style.css` sizes them to match.
